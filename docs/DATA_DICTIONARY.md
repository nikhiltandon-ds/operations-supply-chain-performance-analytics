# Data Dictionary

## orders
Order-level delivery, fulfillment and returns data.

## products
SKU master with category, supplier, pricing and reorder point.

## suppliers
Supplier master and planning attributes.

## inventory_monthly
Monthly SKU/region inventory snapshots with demand and stockout flag.

## Derived outputs
supplier_scorecard.csv, region_performance.csv, monthly_operations.csv, and product_bottlenecks.csv are reproducible analytical outputs.
